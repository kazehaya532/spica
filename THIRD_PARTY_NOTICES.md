## Sky Data

Spica serves a curated copy of the sky data from the pinned Stellarium Web
Engine source tree (`vendor/stellarium-web-engine/apps/test-skydata/`) plus a
deep-sky survey generated locally from OpenNGC. Per-dataset terms:

### Deep-Sky Objects (OpenNGC)

The deep-sky survey (`dso/`) is generated by `scripts/make-dso.mjs` from the
OpenNGC database (`vendor/openngc/NGC.csv` and `vendor/openngc/addendum.csv`,
pinned at commit `da90466031b0372c896588b85be6016c617e205b`), filtered to
objects that are visually notable (Messier/Caldwell/common-named or brighter
than magnitude 13). The generated EPH tiles are an adaptation of OpenNGC and
retain its license:

- OpenNGC database, Copyright Mattia Verga and OpenNGC contributors,
  licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. License text:
  `vendor/openngc/CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt`.
  https://github.com/mattiaverga/OpenNGC

The deep-sky survey therefore remains CC BY-SA 4.0 and is not relicensed
under Spica's AGPL.

### Stars

The bright-star survey (`stars/`, magnitude 7 limit) is generated upstream
from the Hipparcos catalogue (ESA 1997, CDS I/239) and the Kostjuk HD/ADC
star catalogue (CDS IV/27A). CDS catalogue terms permit redistribution for
scientific and educational purposes with proper acknowledgement; Spica uses
them for a noncommercial educational planetarium. Sources:
- https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/ftp/cats/I/239/ReadMe
- https://cdsarc.cds.unistra.fr/ftp/cats/IV/27A/ReadMe
- https://cds.unistra.fr/vizier-org/licences_vizier.html

### Milky Way Panorama

The bundled Milky Way survey at
`vendor/stellarium-web-engine/apps/test-skydata/surveys/milkyway/` is a
HiPS/WebP derivative of Stellarium's full-sky Milky Way texture. The original
panorama is Copyright Axel Mellinger, University of Potsdam, Germany.

Stellarium's credits at the immutable revision immediately preceding this
survey's 2019 release record the following permission in section 4.6:

> Modify and redistribute this image if proper credit to the original image is
> given.

Evidence:

- Original project: https://www.milkywaysky.com/
- Original work: Axel Mellinger, "A Color All-Sky Panorama Image of the Milky
  Way," Publications of the Astronomical Society of the Pacific 121,
  1180-1187 (2009).
- Immutable Stellarium credit record:
  https://github.com/Stellarium/stellarium/blob/af56805c4de6cbc0dbb1e5b93242cd62a938e123/CREDITS.md

Spica republishes the existing upstream tiles without further image
modification. This panorama retains the permission and attribution terms above
and is not relicensed under Spica's AGPL license. The creator's current site
lists separate conditions for other source files, higher resolutions,
commercial use, and educational use; contact `info@milkywaysky.com` before
using the panorama outside the permission recorded by Stellarium.

The Guéreins horizon panorama (`landscapes/guereins/`) is by Fabien Chéreau
and is distributed with Stellarium under GPL-2.0-or-later, as recorded in

### Western Sky Culture

Constellation lines, labels, and data (`skycultures/western/`) are by
Stellarium's team. The upstream declaration in
`skycultures/western/description.md` states: text and data under CC BY-SA
(no version specified upstream; see
https://creativecommons.org/share-your-work/cclicenses/); illustrations
under the Free Art License (https://artlibre.org/licence/lal/en/). Spica
serves only the text/data files (`index.json` and `description.md`); no
illustrations are distributed.

### Removed Development Data

Comet, minor-planet, and satellite element files, the Belarusian sky culture
(whose upstream license declaration was unresolved), the unused Western
constellation illustrations, and unused landscape/sky-culture translations
were removed from the vendored tree because Spica never served them.
